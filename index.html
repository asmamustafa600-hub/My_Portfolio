About

Two years of teaching people, now spent teaching machines.

I started in classrooms, not code editors — as a Spoken English Instructor and Peer Tutor at Govt. Graduate College for Women, Lahore. That work taught me how to spot exactly where someone gets confused, and how to explain the next step so it clicks. I bring that same approach to machine learning: I don't just get a model working, I understand why it works, one concept at a time — gradients, optimizers, architectures — before moving to the next.

I'm currently completing NAVTTC's Data Science and Machine Learning program, where I build, break, and rebuild models in Python — from classical regression to deep learning with TensorFlow and Keras.
